# Doctor HERO: gap matrix against the master specification

**Baseline:** commit `31cb765` ("Doctor HERO final clinical workflow UI"), inspected read-only on 6 Oct 2026 by nine inspectors (shell and home, the consultation queue, patients and documents, the consultation and capture, devices and stethoscope, prescription and completion and the patient app, Mana, thermal, cross-cutting backend and tests). 450 requirement rows were classified with file:line evidence; they are listed in full in the appendix. This page is the owner's master specification (the "FINAL ADAXAI DOCTOR HERO" brief) mapped against what existed **before** this round's implementation.

**Classes:** **A** implemented and working as specified · **B** implemented, needs UI redesign · **C** backend exists, UI missing · **D** missing · **E** hardware-dependent · **F** AI/model-dependent · **G** needs an external integration.

## After this round (Doctor HERO final, ADR 0023)

Every acceptance criterion classed B, C or D below was implemented in this round, **except**: AI-proposed prescription rows (F), handwriting recognition (F), FLIR ONE capture on hardware, a real wearable and stethoscope (E), a real laboratory and DICOM (G), patient self-booking and real-time push (out of the doctor app's scope). The simulated laboratory, band and stethoscope are labelled SIMULATED. What was verified, and what was not (12 outdated e2e specs, no re-run on the real local models, no visual review by eye), is in STATUS.md, section "Doctor HERO final".

## Decisions taken for the implementation (where the inspectors raised open questions)

1. **Today's vocabulary, shared by Home, My Consultations and Mana:** *Yet to see* = today's booked appointments of mine not yet arrived; *Waiting* = arrived today and waiting for the doctor or the nurse (`waiting`, `in_triage`, `ready`); *In consultation* = `in_consultation` (shown in the timeline, counted in Total); *Seen* = visits completed today whose consultation is mine; *Total* = all of today's except cancelled and no-show. Week and month are the facility's calendar week (Monday first) and calendar month to date.
2. **Recording auto-starts** on a *new* Start consultation when the patient's recording consent is on record, the local speech service is ready and the microphone is allowed; otherwise the workspace says plainly why it is not recording and offers the action. Never on a resumed or signed consultation; the synthetic demo never auto-starts.
3. **Sign and complete are separate.** Signing freezes the record (unchanged); **Complete consultation** (the signing doctor) completes the visit and the appointment, is audited, and is what makes the record visible in the Patient App.
4. **Medicines** carry an optional structured duration in days and a start date (default: the consultation's date), signed under hash scheme `adaxai.encounter.v3`; legacy items say "duration not recorded", never "completed".
5. **Service orders** come from a catalogue in configuration; the only provider is a **deterministic simulated lab**, labelled *Simulated* wherever it appears; it never writes results into the patient's investigations and never invents values.
6. **Mana** gets a floating, doctor-only control; deterministic, model-free answers for the queue, counts, arrival times, waiting, triage and orders; voice input transcribed in memory by the local speech model (never stored); spoken answers only with the computer's own voices, off by default; **actions only from the doctor's own fully specified words, proposed, approved by the doctor, then carried out as the doctor's act**. AI-proposed medicine rows are **not** built (no model contract for doses exists: F).
7. **Documents:** a `patient_documents` store (PDF, JPEG, PNG) with category (lab report, imaging report, imaging image, other), modality and study date and an optional typed report text; **surgeries** as `patient_procedures`. No OCR, no DICOM viewer (G), no reading of images by a model (F).
8. **Development banner removed** for every role; simulated, synthetic and unverified marks stay local to their components; the printed "not a valid prescription" note stays.

## Appendix: every inspected requirement

Generated from the inspectors' structured findings. Evidence paths refer to `31cb765`.

### Shell, navigation, Home

Every role (doctor, nurse, admin, patient) uses the same AppShell (apps/web/src/components/shell/AppShell.tsx), with a role-driven Sidebar (NAV record in apps/web/src/lib/nav.ts) and a TopBar that holds patient search (doctor and nurse only), the user's name and Sign out. Every signed-in page, for all roles, gets a full-width "Development build. Synthetic data only…" banner from AppShell.tsx:168-175. docs/DESIGN.md:344 records that as a rule, so removing it needs a doc/ADR change. The doctor sidebar has 7 items: Home, Today's patients (/doctor/queue), Appointments, My consultations (a list of unsigned drafts and signed records), Patients, Thermal, AI review. The spec wants 4: Home, My Consultations, Patients, Thermal. Mana has no global entry point. The backend already supports a general 'clinic' conversation with no patient or encounter (POST /api/mana-assistant/conversations with an empty body), but the UI only shows Mana on the doctor's patient page and in the consultation's ManaArea. DoctorHomePage shows a greeting, four tinted stat cards (In the queue / With a doctor / Completed today / Appointments to come), a Today's schedule card (open visits plus booked appointments only, at most 8 rows, then View all), and a Quick actions grid (New appointment, Register patient, Find a patient, My consultations). The counts are real backend data, added up in the browser from GET /api/visits?mine=true (polled every 5 s, and every call is audited) and GET /api/appointments?scope=today&mine=true. Nothing aggregates by week or month anywhere in the API: the admin overview and Mana's get_today_queue and get_my_consultations only count a single day. The Today/Week/Month selector, week and month counts and trend, a 3-state timeline (Completed / Waiting / Yet to see) that includes completed patients, and the global Mana control are the main gaps. Design tokens, the accessibility groundwork (skip link, drawer focus handling, focus ring, axe tests) and the responsive breakpoints are solid and can be reused. The biggest risk is e2e coupling. About 17 specs start a consultation straight from the Home list (a list item with the patient's name and a 'Start consultation' button). Several specs check the current Home labels and regions, and the phone drawer test clicks the 'Appointments' nav link. The hero journey specs assert that no /api/mana-assistant request is made until Mana is opened, which constrains how a global Mana control may load.

### My Consultations and Start

The doctor's working queue already exists, but under a different name: "Today's patients" at /doctor/queue (TodaysPatientsPage.tsx). It is fed by GET /api/visits?mine=true, which the client polls every 5 s and the server audits on every read as queue.read. It shows real visits with time, patient, status and Start/Continue consultation. "My consultations" (/doctor/consultations, MyConsultationsPage.tsx) means something else today. It lists the doctor's own unsigned drafts from any day plus records signed in the last 30 days (GET /api/encounters/mine). Its main safety job is keeping earlier-day drafts visible, also through EarlierDraftsNotice on Home and on the queue. Starting a consultation works end to end and is solid: ConsultAction calls useStartConsultation, which calls POST /api/visits/:id/encounter (doctor role only, any open visit, concurrency-safe, audited as encounter.start or encounter.resume). It moves the visit to in_consultation and navigates straight to /doctor/encounters/:id. Recording does NOT start automatically. Recorder.tsx is documented "Nothing starts by itself", and e2e tests click "Start recording". The ID chain is visits.id; encounters.visit_id (at most one non-discarded encounter per visit); appointments.visit_id (unique, set at check-in). Encounters have no appointment_id column. Signing currently completes the visit in one step. A database trigger allows in_consultation→completed only once a signed encounter exists, and another trigger then completes the linked appointment. There is no realtime push anywhere: no SSE, no websockets, no staff notifications. The notifications outbox is patient-only, in-app, for appointment events. Patients cannot book appointments themselves. Biggest gaps: 1. My Consultations must become today's chronological queue. Today the queue is sorted by status rank and then urgent/token, and it carries administrative extras: New appointment, day picker, Whole clinic toggle, 5 tabs, and a row menu with vitals/routing/edit/cancel. 2. Recording must auto-start on Start Consultation, gated by the patient's recording consent, with an honest state when it cannot start. 3. After completion the app should return to My Consultations with the patient marked Completed. Today it stays on the signed read view, and the back link says "Back to today's patients". 4. There is no doctor or nurse notification beyond polling, and no patient self-booking.

### Patients, history, documents

Most of the backend for patients and history already exists and works. Search (GET /api/patients) matches name by substring and trigram (GIN index), MRN by prefix and phone by 4 or more digits, and every search is audited. The history read model (GET /api/patients/:id/history) returns allergies, conditions, the medicine list, typed-in investigation results, signed consultations with their diagnoses and prescriptions, latest vitals, and counts of what was left out. Each of these is append-only and audited. There are also a timeline (GET /api/patients/:id/timeline), per-patient thermal captures (GET /api/patients/:id/thermal), clinical photographs (JPEG/PNG/WebP only, consent-gated), stethoscope recordings, and an audited file download (/api/files/:id). Inside the consultation, the History module (module 2) shows a compact fixed-rule summary (HistorySummary) and the full tabbed PastHistoryPanel. The "View profile" side sheet (PatientProfileDrawer) already has Overview, Full history and Documents tabs, which is close to the SUMMARY -> DETAILS -> DOCUMENTS structure the spec asks for. The biggest gaps: (1) The doctor's patient page (/doctor/patients/:id, the shared PatientProfilePage) puts about 12 cards on one screen with no summary first. It mixes in nurse and admin tools (check-in, consents, portal account, appointments, vitals mark-in-error). It does NOT show the medical history at all: no conditions, medicines, investigations or RecordSummary. (2) Surgeries do not exist as data anywhere; they are only free text in the note's pastHistory section. (3) There is no store for MRI, CT, X-ray or lab-report documents. Uploads accept only JPEG/PNG/WebP photos, PDF and DICOM are refused, and there is no category field to tell imaging from lab reports. The only option is the photo type 'clinical_document'. (4) The in-consultation summary is fixed-rule and honestly labelled "not AI-generated". A Mana summary exists only when asked (Ask Mana, "Brief me on this patient"). There are no "View details" or "Documents" links, and the drawer always opens on its Overview tab. Mana can already answer history questions from authorised records, with sources, through get_patient_record. It knows nothing about imaging, documents or surgeries because that data does not exist.

### Consultation and capture

The capture backend is complete and real. Recording runs as browser MediaRecorder parts, sent to POST /api/transcripts/:id/chunks, then a transcription job in the local faster-whisper-small service. Consent is re-checked on every part, timestamps go on one timeline, speakers are assigned by the doctor (no diarizer exists) and audit entries carry counts only. Extraction (extract-rules-v1, local rules) and the V3 local-model draft are also real, with honest streaming progress (tokens, elapsed time, latency from the server) and what was sent recorded in inputsUsed. The biggest gap is the start: recording never starts by itself. Recorder.tsx says so in its own doc comment and an e2e test asserts it. The doctor must open module 1 (closed by default), give consent if it is missing, then click Start recording. The backend already supports auto-start, so this is a UI change. getUserMedia does not need a user gesture in Chromium. The AudioContext level meter will run after an in-app navigation from the Start click, but would start suspended after a page reload. Transcription arrives in 20-second parts, about 3–7 s after each part ends. Shorter parts are a config-only change (the schema allows 5–120 s); about 8–10 s with languageHint 'en' looks safe, while 5 s would run at about 1x real time on this CPU. True word-by-word streaming would need a new engine. Extraction runs only on Stop or a button press, and the button is disabled while recording, even though the server accepts extraction on a live transcript. Auto-extracting after each part is feasible, but every rerun replaces pending points with new ids, so a doctor confirming one mid-review gets a 409. ### Devices and stethoscope

Most of the device and stethoscope backend is already built and safety-hardened. There is a token-authenticated gateway (POST /api/device-ingest/readings, /heartbeat, /auscultations) and a gate on which kinds each device may send: a wearable band may send heart rate, SpO2, temperature and respiratory rate. Blood pressure is always refused from devices (422 kind_not_allowed) and is entered by staff through POST /api/visits/:id/observations. Every reading carries one of five states (live, stale, disconnected, no_data, unavailable), decided by a single readingState() function that both the API and the screen use. Batches are idempotent, consent and assignment windows are enforced, and database triggers guarantee that a row is marked simulated exactly when its device record is a simulator. Simulated values are kept out of visit vitals, history, the patient portal, the models and the rules. In the doctor workspace this shows up as module 3 "Vitals" (VitalsSummary, VitalsTiles and the DeviceReadingsCard poller) and module 4 "Stethoscope" (StethoscopeSummary and AuscultationCard, with browser recording, upload, playback, provenance badges and an amplitude Waveform). Both are honestly labelled SIMULATED / SIMULATED AUDIO / UNVERIFIED / "Not analysed". They are dense and full of technical metadata, so they need redesign rather than a rebuild. The biggest gaps: - **No simulator from the UI.** The device simulator is only a CLI (npm run devices:simulate) that reads a gateway token from .data/sim. It needs an admin-registered simulator record and an attachment first. There is no server-side, per-encounter simulator route, while the thermal simulator does have one (POST /api/patients/:id/thermal/simulate, gated by simulatorAllowed) that can serve as the pattern. - **Simulated stethoscope only via CLI.** Generated tones need a simulator stethoscope record attached to the patient plus the CLI with --recording. - **Blood pressure missing from the main vitals line.** Staff BP appears only in the open module's "Also measured by staff" line. - **No perfusion kind** anywhere: not in the vital_kind pg enum, not in the gate, no hardware. - **Recording is not live.** Stethoscope capture happens in a modal with a seconds counter, no live level meter, and no device streaming. - **Header shows no vitals values.** It shows only a reference-range badge, not "HR 82, BP 118/76, SpO2 98%". Previous visits' values are available from the backend (GET /api/patients/:id/visits latestVitals, GET /api/patients/:id/observations), but no UI uses them and nothing computes an average.

### Prescription, completion, patient app

The prescription is stored per consultation in prescription_items (schema.ts:505-523): drug_name, strength, form, dose, route, frequency, duration and instructions, all free text, plus sort_order. There is no start date, no structured duration in days and no end date. The note sections (chief complaint, history, past history, examination, assessment, plan, follow-up) are text columns on encounters, and diagnoses have their own table. Signing (POST /api/encounters/:id/sign, routes.ts:218-267) works the same way it did in slice 3. It checks completeness with signIssues, takes a canonical snapshot under hash scheme adaxai.encounter.v2, records a SHA-256 content_hash and writes an encounter.sign audit entry that verification checks against. Database triggers freeze the encounter and its items, and corrections go in as hash-chained amendments. In the same transaction it also moves the visit from in_consultation to completed (routes.ts:257-262), and a trigger then completes the linked appointment (0022:256-281). So today signing and completing are one step. The UI says the same: "Complete consultation" opens "Review and sign", and its button "Sign and complete" ends with the toast "Consultation signed and visit completed". Printing is a separate client-side page (/print/encounters/:id) with no side effects. The patient portal shows every signed record as soon as it is signed (portal/routes.ts:55) and reads the same canonical rows, so no copy is made. There is no "today's prescription" view, no medication schedule and no remaining-days count. There is no service, lab or investigation order model anywhere: no table, route, Mana tool or UI. investigation_results only holds results typed in by staff. The biggest gaps are: (1) an explicit POST /encounters/:id/complete, which the existing DB triggers already allow because completion requires a signed encounter; (2) structured duration_days and start_date, which need a migration and a new hash scheme v3 because the signed rows are frozen; (3) a portal endpoint and card for today's prescription with Day N of M; (4) a whole service-order model with a clearly labelled deterministic simulator; (5) a dedicated two-column prescription page reached from a "Generate Prescription" action. A large number of API, seed and e2e tests depend on signing completing the visit.

### Mana

Mana V4 is a real, doctor-only, read-only assistant (apps/api/src/mana/assistant). It has 11 registered read tools (get_current_context, find_patient, get_patient_record, get_patient_visits, get_consultation, get_today_queue, get_recent_visits, get_appointments, get_my_consultations, get_facility_information, consultation_context). The flow for each question is: an LLM plan (or a server-held fixed plan for 13 suggested questions), a permission check before any read, facility-scoped reads with consent filtering, an LLM-written answer, and code checks that tie every statement to its sources. Audit coverage is thorough, SIMULATED data is kept out, and when the model is down it says "Mana is currently unavailable" with no hosted fallback. The V1 deterministic record answers (answers.ts, plus POST /api/patients/:id/mana/questions with generate:false) still exist but have no UI. In the web app, ManaAssistant is mounted in only two places: as the last block of the consultation's Mana area (#mana-chat, closed until opened) and as a companion on the doctor's patient profile. There is no floating or global control, no clinic-scope conversation in the UI, and no voice in or out. The biggest gaps against spec §19–23 are as follows. First, there is no deterministic, LLM-free path: every answer is written by the LLM, any question is refused when the model is down, and Mana shares the single model gate with Generate Prescription (5–7 s per preplanned question on the GPU stack, 30–110 s on CPU). Second, nothing computes "next patient", "waiting longest" or a named patient's arrival time (get_patient_visits does not even output check-in times). Third, there is no orders/lab-status model (only staff-typed investigation_results), no imaging/document store, and no nurse triage note field. Fourth, there is no proposal-and-approve action flow: every write request is refused with MANA_READ_ONLY. Fifth, there is no voice endpoint, although the local speech service is stateless and LocalSpeechTranscriber.transcribe() can be reused for an in-memory clip. Several e2e and API tests encode current behaviour that these changes would touch: Mana located at #mana-chat, input disabled when the model is down, "Who is waiting right now?" going through the planner, and "Prescribe …" being refused.

### Thermal

The V6 thermal backend is complete and works. It has upload, gallery import and live-capture routes (POST /api/patients/:id/thermal), a simulator route (thermal-sim-v1, gated by simulatorAllowed), the flyr radiometric decode in services/thermal, whole-image min/max/mean/SD, rect/ellipse/polygon ROIs with pairs, rules-v1 flags (labelled experimental), a model-status endpoint that always reports MODEL UNAVAILABLE, and a review-gated rule-composed summary (thermal-summary-v1). Provenance is preserved by DB checks and triggers (capture_source, simulated, device_reported, raw file SHA-256). Consent and audit gates are strict. The doctor already has a sidebar entry 'Thermal' (/doctor/thermal), but it is only a facility-wide 'Awaiting review' table (ThermalListPage) with no Upload, no Capture and no patient picker. Upload exists only on the patient record card (PatientThermalCard) and as 'Import' inside the consultation's ThermalWorkspace. Capture (live FLIR or simulator) exists only inside a consultation: the API requires the doctor's own in-progress encounter for live_capture, gallery_import and simulator. Only file_upload can be patient-only. thermal_captures.patient_id is NOT NULL, so a patient is always required. The FLIR boundary is clean (ThermalCameraAdapter, NativeBridgeAdapter, contract v1). The Android bridge is written but not compiled and never run, so in a browser the UI honestly says 'FLIR ONE not connected'. The biggest gaps are four. (1) The Thermal page is not the spec's [Upload][Capture] → viewer → analysis → summary flow. (2) Capture cannot be used outside a consultation. (3) The viewer shows a lot of engineering detail by default: emissivity, extractor, data origin, SHA-256, numeric ROI geometry, labels and flags cards, model-status details. (4) Two association/labelling defects in the consultation: the profile drawer's Upload ignores the current encounter and navigates away, and a rule-composed thermal summary inserted into the note is labelled 'AI-assisted text' with the AI sparkle.

### Cross-cutting backend and tests

The cross-cutting backend is mature and should be reused, not rebuilt. It has 33 checksummed, transactional migrations (0000 to 0032, so the next is 0033), generated with `npm run db:generate` (drizzle-kit). Audit uses one helper, `audit()`, which writes to a SHA-256 hash-chained, append-only `audit_events` table. Routes are default-deny and every route must declare an access rule. Data is scoped by facility, writes are limited to the treating doctor, and refusals are audited. Uploads use a generic `files` table and `storeBytes` (which never overwrites a file), served by `GET /api/files/:id`. Provenance is already kept on the backend for transcription, vitals, stethoscope, thermal and the AI draft, with simulated data flagged and enforced by CHECK constraints (section 25 is essentially A). The biggest gaps are these: 1. The digital pen (section 11) does not exist at all. There is no ink model, storage or UI. The only trace is an unused `WritingPadCard` placeholder, and `history.spec.ts` asserts that the pad is absent. Storing it needs a small new table, while the bytes can go in `files`. 2. Nothing is realtime. There is no SSE or WebSocket. The web app polls instead: the queue every 5 s (each poll is audited as `queue.read`), appointments every 60 s, the AI draft every 1 s and the context every 15 s. The notification outbox goes to patients only, in-app (the portal). 3. Patients cannot book from the Patient App. 4. Mana has no actions to audit, because it is read-only. 5. Signing the record also completes the visit, in one transaction. Many API and e2e tests depend on this, so separating "Complete consultation" from signing (17A/B) is the biggest test-churn item. The e2e suite has 30 spec files and 119 test definitions, which is about 246 runs across the desktop and mobile projects. A full run takes about 13 to 25 minutes on 2 workers, with 13 to 19 failures caused by this machine's environment stalls. About 12 specs depend on a 'Start consultation' button inside a list row on the landing page (Home), and 9 specs depend on 'Complete consultation' then 'Review and sign' then 'Sign and complete'. The five-module rail test ids, 'Analyze with Mana', 'Today’s schedule' / 'Today’s patients' and the greeting are also asserted. No test checks the development banner.
